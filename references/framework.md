# Three lenses: interpretations and limits

These concepts are interpretive lenses, not scientifically established universal laws. Alan Shao publicly articulated his version of 黄毛思维; that does not establish authorship of the wider internet phrase.

## 孙学：strategy, learning and optionality

- Examine opportunity costs, information quality, resource constraints, and downside protection.
- Favor learning and reversible tests when uncertainty is high; recognize when commitment is worthwhile.
- Use AI to find information gaps, compare scenarios and challenge assumptions. Do not outsource final judgment.
- Beware survivorship bias: one public figure's path is not a replicable formula for everyone.

Question: Which path preserves the most worthwhile future options relative to its risks and costs?

## 黄毛思维：agency, boundaries and initiative

- Identify the user's genuine preferences and purpose.
- Communicate needs and boundaries directly and respectfully.
- Separate imagined disapproval from concrete external costs.
- Accept credible criticism without surrendering autonomy.
- Respect others' choices, boundaries and consent.

Question: If fear of judgment mattered less, what **respectful** action would I take?

Failure mode: confusing confidence with manipulation, entitlement, recklessness or arrogance.

## 薄肌理论：fitness and sustainable execution

- Recognize the physique goal as an individual aesthetic preference, not a moral rule.
- Encourage safe progressive strength training, appropriate aerobic activity, adequate food, rest and sleep.
- Extend the framework to sustainable personal capacity as **this Skill's adaptation**, not as a direct quote or necessarily Alan's original definition.
- Consider functional wellbeing, adherence, and recovery as well as appearance.

Question: Can my body, time and energy sustainably support this plan?

Failure mode: injury, excessive restriction, body-image obsession or extreme schedules.

## Integrate the perspectives

Strategy asks **where to invest and why**. Agency asks **who chooses and what action to take**. Sustainable capacity asks **whether the plan is repeatable**. Weight lenses according to relevance rather than giving all three equal airtime.
